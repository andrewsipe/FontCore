House conventions

Naming rules

The tools read the style from the filename and build the name records from it. These are the owner's conventions, not an industry standard; the reasons are in DECISIONS.md.

What each record holds

RecordHolds
Family (ID 1)The family plus every style word except Regular, Italic and Bold. Variable fonts keep Variable.
Style (ID 2)One of Regular, Italic, Bold, Bold Italic.
Full name (ID 4)Family and style, and it keeps Regular.
Typographic style (ID 17)The whole style. It always has a weight.

Words and compounds

  • Weight compounds are one word: Semibold, Extralight. Smallcaps is the one exception to keeping capitals.
  • Width compounds are joined and keep their capitals: SemiCondensed, XXCondensed, SemiMono.
  • The style order is Family {Optical}-{Width} {Weight} {Slope}.
  • There is no list of slope words and no list of optical sizes. Position and the user's own list decide.

Regular and a missing weight

The tool adds Regular only when the style is empty, or holds only width words or only Italic. Any other style with no known weight word keeps its text and prints a note, because the unknown word may itself be the weight. A filename with no hyphen is skipped under -fp: there is no style to read.

To answer the question yourself, pass --weight-word WORD to name the weight for every style that has none (none adds nothing and silences the note), and --weight-position last|first to choose where it goes. A style that already has a weight is never touched.

Style (ID 2): the name wins

  1. NameThe word Bold makes Bold. The word Italic makes Italic.
  2. FlagsOnly when the style says nothing at all, the font's Italic and Bold flags decide.
  3. AngleOnly when the style says nothing or names a slope (Oblique, Slanted, Backslant, Cursive), an italic angle of 2 degrees or more, forward or back, makes Italic.
A slanted font named Regular stays Regular. Italic is a linked alternative to a Regular. A script with nothing to link to isn't an Italic, and the tool prints a note instead of guessing. The font's Italic and Bold flags follow ID 2.

Known splits are not fixed here

Splits such as S Sm (ScreenSmart) or 3 D are handled by the NameID find-and-replace step, not by the rules above.
